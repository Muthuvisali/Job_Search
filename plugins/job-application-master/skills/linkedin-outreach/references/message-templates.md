# Outreach templates

Fill every `<…>`. Pull strengths only from the tailored resume. Cut anything generic.
Plain text (no Markdown) for email bodies.

## Email — recruiter

Subject: <Job Title> application — <Candidate Name>

Hi <First name>,

I just applied for the <Job Title> role at <Company> (<Job URL>) and wanted to reach out
directly. <One sentence: most relevant experience, e.g. "For the past 5 years I've led B2B
analytics products at Acme, most recently growing self-serve adoption 40%.">

What drew me to this role is <specific JD/company detail>. It lines up closely with
<second concrete strength from the resume>.

I've attached my resume, and you can see examples of my work here: <portfolio_url>.
Would you be open to a quick chat about the role?

Thanks,
<signature>

## Email — hiring manager

Subject: <Job Title> on your team — <Candidate Name>

Hi <First name>,

I saw you're hiring a <Job Title> at <Company>. <Specific hook about their team/product from the
JD or company page.>

A quick snapshot of why I think I could help:
- <Strength 1 with metric from resume>
- <Strength 2 with metric from resume>

My resume is attached and my portfolio is at <portfolio_url>. I've also applied through
<Company>'s careers page. If it's useful, I'd welcome 15 minutes to hear what success in the
first six months looks like.

Best,
<signature>

## Email — peer / referral

Subject: Question about <team> at <Company>

Hi <First name>,

I'm applying for the <Job Title> role on <team> at <Company> and noticed you work on <area>.
<One genuine line about their work or shared background.>

I'd really value 10 minutes of your perspective on the team. In case it's helpful, my resume is
attached and my portfolio is <portfolio_url>.

Thank you,
<signature>

## LinkedIn connection note (≤ 200 chars)

- Recruiter: `Hi <First> — I just applied for <Company>'s <Short Title> role. <5–8 word strength>. Would love to connect! — <Name>`
- Hiring manager: `Hi <First> — I'm excited about the <Short Title> opening on your team. I've <5–8 word strength>; would love to connect. — <Name>`
- Peer: `Hi <First> — I'm exploring the <Short Title> role at <Company> and admire your work on <area>. Would love to connect. — <Name>`
