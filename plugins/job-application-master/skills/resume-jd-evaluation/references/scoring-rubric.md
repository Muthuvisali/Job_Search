# Fit scoring rubric (0–100)

| Criterion | Max | How to score |
|---|---|---|
| **Required skills & tools** | 35 | `35 × (credited must-haves / total must-haves)`. Full credit = demonstrated in an experience bullet; half = only listed in Skills or used briefly. |
| **Experience level** | 20 | Compare relevant years and scope to the JD. Meets/exceeds = 20; 1 year short = 14; 2 years short = 8; 3+ short = 3. Large overshoot for a junior role = 12 (flag over-qualification). |
| **Responsibilities overlap** | 15 | How many of the JD's core responsibilities the user has done before, at similar scope. |
| **Domain / industry** | 15 | Same industry/product type = 15; adjacent (e.g. B2B SaaS → fintech SaaS) = 9; unrelated = 3. |
| **Nice-to-haves** | 10 | `10 × (credited preferred items / total preferred items)`; 10 if none listed. |
| **Logistics** | 5 | Location/work mode and seniority match the user's config = 5; partial = 2; mismatch = 0. |

## Hard caps (apply after summing)

| Condition | Cap |
|---|---|
| Required license, clearance, or degree that is legally/explicitly mandatory and absent | 45 |
| JD states no visa sponsorship and the user needs sponsorship | 40 |
| Fewer than half of must-haves credited | 65 |
| Role is two or more levels away from the user's target seniority | 60 |

Record any cap applied in `Evaluation Notes`, e.g. `Capped at 65: 3/7 must-haves`.

## Bands

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 85–100 | Strong fit — apply and do outreach first |
| 75–84 | Good fit — shortlisted |
| 60–74 | Stretch — not shortlisted by default |
| < 60 | Poor fit |

## Breakdown string format

`Skills 28/35 · Exp 14/20 · Resp 12/15 · Domain 9/15 · Pref 6/10 · Logistics 5/5`
